# SocialFlow OS — Editable Demo Video Plan

## Goal
Create a neat, clear product walkthrough that a business owner can play for clients or team members. The narration is paced at a natural 1× speaking rate, with no accelerated voice.

## Audience
- Social media managers
- Agencies and business owners
- New SocialFlow OS users

## Narrative
SocialFlow OS turns a scattered content workflow into a controlled path: select the client, plan the campaign, create content, attach media, review, choose an account, schedule, and monitor.

## Visual language
- 1920 × 1080, 30 fps
- Dark navy and violet SocialFlow palette
- White typography, cyan and violet accents
- Actual application screenshots in clean framed cards
- Large step numbers and short on-screen instructions
- Captions throughout

## Scenes
1. **Welcome** — SocialFlow OS promise and workflow overview.
2. **Dashboard** — Explain the command center.
3. **Clients** — Keep brands, accounts, and campaigns isolated.
4. **Campaigns and AI Workspace** — Enter goal, idea, dates, platforms, content types, and automation mode.
5. **Content Studio and Media** — Edit copy, attach media, preview images/videos, download when needed.
6. **Human Approval** — Review before real scheduling or publishing.
7. **Social Accounts** — Select a client and use official Connect Account; never paste passwords or tokens.
8. **Calendar** — Select the exact connected account, date, time, and timezone.
9. **Publishing Queue** — Monitor queued, retrying, failed, and published work.
10. **Safe operating rules** — Mock Mode first, explicit accounts, review outputs, inspect errors.
11. **Closing** — Simple explanation to share with others.

## Truthfulness boundary
The video demonstrates the application workflow. It does not claim that live provider OAuth or publishing has been completed where owner configuration and real test accounts are unavailable.

## Editable deliverables
- `narration.txt` — voice script
- `timeline.json` — scene timing and captions
- `render_video.py` — editable video source
- `screenshots/` — replaceable application screenshots
- `voiceover.mp3` — narration audio
- `SocialFlow-OS-Step-by-Step-Demo.mp4` — finished video
