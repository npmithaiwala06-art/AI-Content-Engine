# Phases 15–21 — Analytics, AI Learning and Reports

- Published Mock jobs generate deterministic local reach, impression, view, engagement, click, follower and engagement-rate metrics.
- The Analytics dashboard provides date/client/platform filters, KPIs, time-series, platform comparison, top-post and content-type views.
- **Analyse with ChatGPT** produces a structured manual prompt. The importer validates the returned JSON and stores findings, strong/weak topics and formats, posting guidance, strategy and future ideas.
- New content prompts automatically include up to three recent learning records, completing the AI improvement loop without an AI API.
- Weekly, monthly, campaign, platform and client report snapshots are stored in SQLite.
- Desktop exports create valid local PDF and CSV files under the application-data `reports` folder and reveal them in Finder.
